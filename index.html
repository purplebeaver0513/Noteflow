Keyboard shortcuts & how to play

Find the target

Choose a range, listen, and select the matching note and octave. Replay the target as often as you like. New note starts another round.

Switch to Free play to explore chords without scoring. Record take captures your virtual piano performance, not your microphone. Recording and playback never count as guesses.

Computer keyboard layouts

Select Q / Z piano layout above the piano. Q W E R T Y U plays C3 D3 E3 F3 G3 A3 B3; 2 3 5 6 7 plays C♯3 D♯3 F♯3 G♯3 A♯3. The lower row Z X C V B N M plays C4 D4 E4 F4 G4 A4 B4, with S D G H J for its sharps. Hold Shift to raise new notes by two octaves (C5–B6). Release Shift to return. Notes already held keep their original pitch until released. Mouse and touch always play the displayed note. Your layout choice is remembered on this device.

Original shortcuts

Middle octave: t C4 y D4 u E4 i F4 o G4 p A4 a B4. Hold Shift on a sharp’s mapped key. All C2–B6 mappings are retained.

Space repeats the target when a control is not focused. On the piano, arrow keys move between notes; Enter or Space plays the focused key. Scroll sideways to reach C1–C8.

Session results and takes stay here until you close or reload the app. Switching tabs stops sound and ends an active take, keeping it available for playback.

Your music workspace

  1. Choose the source and output instrument.

    Choose what you are playing on the left, then select the instrument you want the score written for above the sheet music. Flute uses treble clef, trombone uses bass, viola uses alto, and piano uses both treble and bass. Pitches stay at concert pitch.

  2. Record or upload audio.

    Use Melodies + chords for simultaneous notes, or Single-note melody for faster analysis. Allow microphone access and leave about half a second of room noise before playing. Record or upload up to 2 minutes.

  3. Listen and refine.

    Choose a noise-reduction level, then compare Original and Cleaned audio. Play the score and use Edit notes to fix pitches or chords. Change the analysis settings and select Retranscribe to try the same recording again.

  4. Keep your music.

    Export PDF sheet music, MIDI, MusicXML, or synthesized WAV. Download a project to reopen it later. Download app gives you a portable offline copy.

  5. Train your ear in PPitch.

    Listen to a target, then choose the matching note and octave. Switch to Free play to explore chords without scoring. Record take captures the virtual piano keys you play; it does not record your microphone. Stop ends a take or its playback. Open the keyboard-shortcuts guide in the PPitch tab for both keyboard layouts.

Audio processing happens in your browser. Your latest score is saved on this device when browser storage is available; recordings are kept only until you leave.
